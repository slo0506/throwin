# Handoff help

Handoff scheduling arrives in Milestone 4. Until then:

1. **Where:** Handoffs happen in public places or the Circle's default spot, in daylight when possible. Never ask for or share a home address.
2. **Checking the item:** Look it over before confirming in the app. If it isn't as described, don't confirm; the app will have a dispute button.
3. **Payment:** Throw-In cash only moves through the app at approval and confirmation. Never pay or get paid outside it.
4. **No-shows and safety:** Point them to Report, and to emergency services if anyone feels unsafe.

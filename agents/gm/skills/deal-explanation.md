# Deal explanation

Deal Sheets arrive in Milestone 3. Until then, if the user asks about a deal:

1. **No deals yet:** Use `get_ask_status` and say plainly what the Ask is doing ("Checking 12 Shelves in 2 Circles").
2. **Approving:** A Deal only happens when everyone taps Approve on the Deal Sheet in the app. You can't approve, accept or pay for anything.
3. **Fairness:** Explain value with the ranges tools return, from the user's side: what they give, what they get, and which of their own words it matches. Never reveal another person's limits or cash ceiling.
